SAMPLE STUDY (fictional data for testing the lab)

TITLE
Effect of a 12 week health education programme on blood pressure control among hypertensive adults attending a primary health centre in Lagos

STUDY DESIGN
Community or field trial

OBJECTIVES (paste one per line)
To describe the sociodemographic characteristics and blood pressure profile of the participants
To compare mean systolic blood pressure between the intervention and control groups after 12 weeks
To determine the change in knowledge score before and after the health education
To determine the association between education level and blood pressure control
To identify factors independently associated with blood pressure control

DATA ANALYSIS PLAN (paste as one block)
Descriptive statistics will be presented as frequencies, percentages, means and standard deviations. Student's t test will be used to compare sbp_followup between group. Student's t test will be used to compare sbp_followup across education levels. Paired t test will be used to compare knowledge_pre and knowledge_post. Chi square test will be used for the association between stroke_history and group. Pearson correlation will be used for age and sbp_baseline. Logistic regression will be used to identify predictors of bp_controlled. The level of significance will be set at 0.05.

WHAT TO EXPECT FROM THE PLAN CHECK
The plan was written with deliberate weaknesses so you can see the checker work:
1. Student's t test for education levels (4 groups) should be flagged: ANOVA is better.
2. Chi square for stroke_history and group should be flagged: expected counts are small, so Fisher's exact test is better.
3. The plan never mentions checking normality, so a Gap note should appear.
4. The plan never mentions confidence intervals or effect sizes, so another Gap note should appear.
